How Jal Parakh works · यह कैसे काम करता है

Four steps

  1. Strip reader (Gemini vision). Finds the strip and the colour chart, matches each pad, estimates the value and how sure it is. Blurry photo, no chart or low confidence → it asks for a retake.
  2. Rules engine (no AI). Compares each value with BIS IS 10500:2012. Health parameters above their limit → Unsafe; above the ideal but tolerable → Use with caution; all fine → Safe on tested parameters. Water is never called safe on a low-confidence reading.
  3. District risk agent. Official CGWB data on fluoride and arsenic districts (PIB, 24 Mar 2022) and state-level nitrate, iron and salinity counts (PIB, 29 Nov 2021) tell you what else to test for.
  4. Action agent (Gemini, grounded). Cheapest effective fix, health warnings, when to see a doctor, in Hindi and English, plus a complaint draft for PHED / Jal Jeevan Mission that you copy and send yourself.

Important · ज़रूरी

This is not a laboratory test. Home strips are approximate and cannot detect bacteria. Confirm any worrying result at your district water testing laboratory (many are open to the public at a nominal fee — see the JJM Citizen Corner).
